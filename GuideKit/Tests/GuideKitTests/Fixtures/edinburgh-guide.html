Notes

Weather

Expect 1 to 9°C, short days and rain on at least one of them. A waterproof and good shoes matter more than anything smart.

Light

The sun rises after eight and sets before four. Blue hour is the best time for the Christmas lights.

Backup plan

If it pours, swap Calton Hill for the street food hall and save the views for a dry hour.

Rules of the trip

Eat when you're hungry, not when the plan says. One pub an evening is plenty. Stairs are fine; hurry is not.

Good to know

  • Late-November Scotland can be cold, wet and windy. A waterproof layer and comfortable shoes beat a formal outfit. Source: example.com
  • Sunrise roughly 08:03-08:07, sunset around 15:52-15:54 across the visit. Plan outdoor photos before dark. Source: timeanddate.com
  • New Town Test Apartment: check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00, no lift, so expect stairs. Luggage store on Rose Street, 09:00-17:00 daily. Source: example.com
  • Stockbridge Market runs on Sundays, 10:00-16:00. Source: edinburgh.org
  • Edinburgh's Christmas runs 15 Nov 2025 to 4 Jan 2026; the main market is generally 10:00-22:00. Source: edinburgh.org
  • Rain? Edinburgh Street Food indoors, then the Guildford Arms and a browse for Scottish groceries. Still an excellent day. Source: example.com
  • 999 for emergencies. 111 (NHS 24) for urgent medical advice that is not an emergency. Source: example.com

Tasting

Lorne sausage

Square sausage. Brilliant in a morning roll, especially with a tattie scone.

Haggis, neeps & tatties

Peppery, savoury haggis with mashed turnip and potatoes. Order it cooked, not as a souvenir.

Scotch pie or bridie

Warm meat pastries from a bakery, ideal when the air is cold.

Black pudding

Look for Stornoway black pudding, or try it as part of a Scottish breakfast.

Cullen skink

Rich smoked-haddock, potato and onion soup. Made for a wintry day.

Scottish tablet

Crumbly, caramel-like and intensely sweet. Buy a small packet first.

Cranachan

Cream, toasted oats and raspberries, sometimes with whisky and honey.

A locally brewed pint

Scottish cask ale or an Edinburgh lager. It counts as cultural research.
