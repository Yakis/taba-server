# TABA iOS app

The app plan (milestones M2 to M4) and its design guide live in the app repo, `taba-ios` (private), next
to this one: `../taba-ios/docs/PLAN.md` and `../taba-ios/docs/DESIGN.md`.

GuideKit, the model the app renders, stays in this repo. In M2 step 1 it moves into its own package folder
(`GuideKit/`) so the app can depend on it without the server's dependencies.
